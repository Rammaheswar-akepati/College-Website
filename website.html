About Our College

Marwadi University (MU) is a private university located in Rajkot, Gujarat, India. It was established on 9 May 2016 by the Marwadi Education Foundation through The Gujarat Private Universities (Amendment) Act, 2016. As of 2017, it offers 54 different courses. It is graded A+ by NAAC.

The campus is located on 52 acres of land, having a distance of nearly 40 minutes from railway and airports. The university comprises eight multi-storey buildings.Laboratories, research facilities, student clubs, sports club and college cafeteria are available.

100% Scholarship on Tuition Fee: Students are given 100% waiver on Tuition Fee. They only need to spend on accommodation and food. Apart from 100% Tuition Fees Waiver, the President has also given his consent to give additional President Scholarship.
